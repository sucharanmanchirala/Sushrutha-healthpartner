🛡️ What Susrutha is — and is not

  • Is: a health-literacy and navigation tool. It summarises public health guidance and helps you reach care faster.
  • Is not: a doctor, a diagnosis, or a prescription. Symptoms overlap across many illnesses, and only a clinician can examine, test and confirm.
  • Is: transparent. Every condition card names its source (WHO, CDC, NHS, MedlinePlus) so you can verify it.
  • Is not: a data collector. No account, no database, no tracking of your health inputs.

Facility names and areas are real; map pins are approximate. Always call the hospital to confirm the entrance, OPD timings, and that the test you need is available the same day. In an emergency, go to the nearest emergency department rather than travelling across the city.
